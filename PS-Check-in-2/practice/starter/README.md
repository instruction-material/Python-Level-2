# Check-In 2 practice starter

The starter counts unique letters after changing case and discarding spaces, digits, and punctuation. Banana, banana! gives three letters. Add a second prompt and count words in the paragraph. Convert to lowercase and replace nonletters with spaces, then split on whitespace and turn the word list into a set. Red blue red. gives two unique words out of three. Red,blue red also gives two out of three because punctuation separates adjacent words.
