# Named-Card Twenty-One starter

Count Jack, Queen and King as ten. Count every ace as one. If an ace is present and adding ten keeps the total at or below 21, add ten. Ace and 6 total 17, then Ace, 6 and 8 total 15. Use the same helper for each hand and have the dealer stop at a best total of 17 or higher.

In practice mode, hit twice and then stay. The player totals are 17, 15 and 19. The dealer draws from 16 to 18. Check dual two-card 21 as a tie.
