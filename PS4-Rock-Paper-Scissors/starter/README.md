# Rock, Paper, Scissors starter

Player 1 and Player 2 each enter rock, paper, or scissors. The starter trims spaces, accepts mixed case, and detects a tie from matching valid moves. Enter Rock and rock to see the working first case.

Complete the three Player 1 win cases: rock beats scissors, scissors beats paper, and paper beats rock. The remaining valid unequal pairs are Player 2 wins. Check all nine valid pairs and enter lizard for a move to check that the round ends without a winner.
