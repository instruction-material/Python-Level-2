# Joe's Donuts Opening Day starter

Enter a positive whole-number line position. The starter gives position 1 an extra offer and prints the year-round offer. Add one opening-day tier. Positions 1–12 get a free dozen, 13–200 can buy the first dozen for $1, position 144 gets a free month in place of its tier, 201–500 receive 50% off, and 501 and above get the third donut free with two purchases. Position 1 also gets free donuts for a year. All positive positions get the year-round 13th-free offer with a dozen.
