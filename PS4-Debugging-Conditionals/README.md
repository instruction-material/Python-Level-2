List every item the player can afford individually and give the leftover tickets for each possible purchase. The player can afford matching T-shirts by lending six tickets to a friend and buying one T-shirt with the remaining balance.

Check 9 and 10, 74 and 75, 249 and 250, 255 and 256, and 349 and 350. With 256 tickets, the lollipop, action figure, and T-shirt are affordable individually. Buying a T-shirt alone leaves 6 tickets. Lending all 6 and buying a T-shirt leaves 0. The stuffed animal costs 350.
